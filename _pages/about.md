Research Interests
======
- **Medical image analysis**: segmentation and detection across X-ray, ultrasound, CT, and MRI
- **Vision foundation models**: adapting foundation models and prompt-based segmentation to medical images
- **Multi-modality**: text–image models for medical imaging
- **Computer vision**: image segmentation, object detection, and pose estimation
